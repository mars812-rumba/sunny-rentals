# Approved lessons — Sunny

Curated rules from accepted owner feedback, not automatic model training. New
suggestions remain proposals until approved. Preserve the distinction between
project preferences and portable editorial lessons.

- Project: write as our team, not a third-party reviewer of Sunny. Use «мы передаём
  машину…» only when the handover terms are confirmed.
- Project, owner confirmation 22 October 2026: travel within Phuket, Krabi and
  Phang Nga needs no prior approval and insurance is valid there. The territory
  questions blocking the Krabi guide are answered. Do not extend this to other
  provinces, ferries or promises of insurance without exclusions/excess.
- Project, owner confirmation 26 October 2026: Sunny cars may not travel to
  Bangkok. The Bangkok article was cancelled in favour of an insurance explainer;
  do not promote that route or a Bangkok handover as a Sunny service.
- General: do not invent employees, personal experience or customer reviews to sound
  human; use concrete, helpful explanations instead.
- Project: do not introduce replacement/substitute-car promises. Distinguish booking
  prepayment, refundable deposit and insurance excess.
- Project: pricing guides need verified class/term examples, with season and evidence
  date, not just a list of possible expenses.
- General: published articles must be discoverable from their collection. Language
  switching preserves the article when a translation exists.
- General: avoid walls of text; use short sections and purposeful visuals. Real
  inventory photos and generated illustrative scenes are not interchangeable.
- Project: use distinct, topic-led covers rather than repeating a cafe/key still
  life. The car-vs-taxi article should visually compare those choices. Public
  captions refer briefly to the article title; technical provenance stays recorded.
- Project: compact article tables must preserve whole words and amounts on phones;
  use local horizontal scrolling instead of crushing columns or splitting digits.
- Project: Russian approval comes before English preparation. Adapt only the
  owner's approved Russian revision, never a parallel unfinished draft. Preserve
  prices and low-season caveats, localize wording/humor and links, then run the
  English review/browser checks. Approval of Russian is not publication permission.
