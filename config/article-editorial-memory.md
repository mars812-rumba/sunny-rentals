# Approved lessons — Sunny

Curated rules from accepted owner feedback, not automatic model training. New
suggestions remain proposals until approved. Preserve the distinction between
project preferences and portable editorial lessons.

- Project: write as our team, not a third-party reviewer of Sunny. Use «мы передаём
  машину…» only when the handover terms are confirmed.
- General: do not invent employees, personal experience or customer reviews to sound
  human; use concrete, helpful explanations instead.
- Project: do not introduce replacement/substitute-car promises. Distinguish booking
  prepayment, refundable deposit and insurance excess.
- Project: pricing guides need verified class/term examples, with season and evidence
  date, not just a list of possible expenses.
- General: published articles must be discoverable from their collection. Language
  switching preserves the article when a translation exists.
- General: avoid walls of text; use short sections and purposeful visuals. Real
  inventory photos and generated illustrative scenes are not interchangeable.
